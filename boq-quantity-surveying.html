BOQ & quantity surveying

Home Drafters Pte Ltd provides bills of quantities (BOQ), construction quantity take-offs, cost estimates and quantity surveying support for building and civil projects in Lautoka and across Fiji.

Enquire about this service

BOQ preparation and cost estimating

A well-prepared BOQ helps describe the work scope, measure quantities and support pricing and tender review. We can discuss the drawings, specifications and project information available and confirm the appropriate scope of measurement.

Quantity take-offs

Measurement of building and civil work quantities from supplied drawings and specifications.

Bills of quantities

Preparation and organisation of measured work items to support pricing and tender documentation.

Construction estimates

Cost estimates and budget support based on the available scope, quantities and project information.

Tender support

Assistance with reviewing work items, comparing pricing information and clarifying measured scope.

Quantity surveying support

Quantity surveying involves measuring, assessing and managing construction costs across a project. The level of support can be tailored to the information and stage of the project.

Information to send for a quotation

  • Architectural, structural and civil drawings, where available
  • Specifications and schedules
  • Project location and building type
  • Any required BOQ format or trade breakdown
  • Deadline and intended use of the estimate
